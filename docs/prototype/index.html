Built for rural finance

Simple enough for daily use. Structured enough for real growth.

RFUND helps people save daily, weekly, or monthly, then withdraw at the end of a quarterly, bi-yearly, or yearly tenure. It also gives rural entrepreneurs and smallholder farmers a clear path to apply for responsible, collateral-free financing.

A

Digital Ajo

A modern version of traditional rotating savings groups, using mobile payments, USSD codes, and trusted local agents for secure thrift collection.

  • Daily, weekly, or monthly contributions
  • Quarterly, bi-yearly, or yearly payout tenure
  • Member records and payment receipts
L

Rural Loans

Small financing for village shops, tailoring businesses, food processing, petty trading, and other local income-generating work.

  • Simple loan application
  • Business-purpose review
  • Repayment plans matched to income
F

FarmerCash

Collateral-free seasonal loans for smallholder farmers who need support for seeds, fertilizer, labor, harvest, transport, and storage.

  • Farm-cycle repayment options
  • Cooperative and agent verification
  • Production-focused support
